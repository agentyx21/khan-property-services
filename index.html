The approach

One team, one contact, one written record.

Most properties are looked after by a rotating cast of contractors, each with their own way of quoting, scheduling and reporting. We bring that work into one team with one way of working — so nothing falls between trades, and every job leaves a record.

  1. 01

    A single point of contact

    One named coordinator for your property, from the first walkthrough to the final sign-off. You never have to explain the building twice.

  2. 02

    A written scope before work begins

    Every job starts with a scope of work: what will be done, what is excluded, the sequence and how access will work. Any change is recorded and approved the same way.

  3. 03

    Updates on a schedule you agree

    Before work starts, we agree how and how often you will hear from us — and keep to it, whether the job runs a day or a season.

  4. 04

    Photo-documented completion

    Each job closes with a report: what was done, photographs of the finished work, the closed punch list and anything to watch.

How we work

Documentation

What you receive, in writing.

Accountability is easier to promise than to show. These are the documents that show it — and they stay in your property record, so the next job starts from what the last one found.

  • Scope of work

    What will be done and what will not, the sequence, access arrangements and the estimate — agreed before work begins.

  • Change record

    Any change to scope, cost or timing, written down and approved by you before it goes ahead.

  • Progress updates

    On the schedule agreed at the start: what is done, what is next and anything that needs your decision.

  • Completion report

    Photographs of the finished work, materials and products used, the closed punch list and anything to watch.

Illustrative format. Every report reflects the actual job.

Handing over a property

Before you hand over the keys.

Giving a contractor access to a home, a rental or a business premises is a real decision. These are the questions owners and managers put to us first — with straight answers.

Read how we work
Who will I deal with day to day?

A named coordinator for your property, from the first walkthrough to the final report. They hold the scope, the schedule and the property record, so you never have to explain the building twice.

How are keys and access handled?

Access is agreed before the first visit: who holds keys, lockbox or concierge details, notice for occupants and working hours. Every key, code and access device is logged out and back in.

What do I approve before anything starts?

A scope of work: what will be done and what is excluded, the sequence, how occupants will be handled, and the estimate. Small repairs inside a limit you set are confirmed by email instead.

How will I know what is happening once work is underway?

We agree how and how often you hear from us before work starts — by email or through your own system — and keep to it. Anything that needs your decision comes to you with the options laid out.

What if the work uncovers something unexpected?

That item stops. We photograph it, explain what has changed and set out the effect on cost and timing in writing. Work on it continues only once you approve.

What will I have when the job is finished?

A completion report: photographs of the finished work, the materials and products used, the closed punch list and anything to watch — filed to your property record for next time.

Is there work you will not take on?

Yes. Work that requires a licensed specialist — gas, electrical panels and new wiring, refrigerant-based heating and cooling, structural engineering and hazardous materials — is outside our scope. We will tell you when something falls into one of those areas.
