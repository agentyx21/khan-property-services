Questions

Questions commercial premises often ask.

Who else we work with
Do you carry out full commercial fit-outs?

We handle finish-level work: flooring, decoration, joinery repairs, fixtures within the existing layout and make-good. Work needing engineering, fire-system changes or new electrical and mechanical services is outside our scope.

Can work happen outside opening hours?

Where it is agreed in the scope, yes. Working hours are set out in the schedule so everyone — including neighbors in shared buildings — knows what to expect.

We own a building with a shop below and apartments above. Is that a fit?

Yes. Mixed-use buildings need commercial and residential thinking at once. Tell us about the whole building and we will scope each part accordingly.
