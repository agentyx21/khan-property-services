About

A property services company built around accountability.

Khan Property Services exists for one reason: the people who own and manage property deserve one accountable team — not a list of contractors to coordinate themselves.

Why we are set up this way

Properties fail at the gaps between contractors.

A typical property is looked after by a handful of separate firms: one for repairs, another for painting, someone else for the gutters, a different crew every turnover. Each quotes differently, schedules differently and — if they report at all — reports differently.

The result is familiar to most owners and managers. Nobody holds the whole picture. Small issues are noticed by one trade and left for another. Paperwork lives in inboxes, if it exists at all.

We set the company up the other way round. One team takes responsibility for the property across planned maintenance, repairs, renovation, turnovers, pre-sale preparation and exterior upkeep — with one coordinator, one way of scoping and one record.

Principles

Five principles that shape every job.

They are deliberately practical. Each one shows up in a document you receive or a step in our process.

  1. 01

    Write it down first

    Scope, changes and completion are all documented. If it is not written down, it has not been agreed — which protects you as much as us.

  2. 02

    One point of contact

    One coordinator holds the scope, the schedule and the record for your property, so you never have to explain it twice.

  3. 03

    Evidence over assumption

    Walkthroughs, photographs and condition notes come before recommendations. Priorities are set on what we can see.

  4. 04

    Plan rather than react

    Planned work is calmer, cheaper to organize and kinder to occupants than emergencies. We help you move work from one column to the other.

  5. 05

    Leave a better record than we found

    Every job adds to the property’s history, so the next decision — ours or anyone else’s — starts from facts.

Clear boundaries

What we are — and what we are not.

Being accountable starts with being clear about where our responsibility begins and ends.

We are

  • A property services company that plans, maintains, repairs and renews homes, rental units and small commercial premises in Virginia and Florida.
  • One accountable team for building fabric, fixtures, fittings and finishes.
  • A contractor that works for owners directly, or for the property managers who act for them.
  • Committed to agreed scopes, scheduled updates and photo-documented completion on every job.

We are not

  • A property manager. We do not collect rent, place tenants or manage leases.
  • A substitute for licensed specialists in gas, electrical panels and wiring, refrigerant-based heating and cooling, or structural engineering.
  • A home inspection service. Our condition walkthroughs plan work; they are not inspection reports.
  • A firm that starts work beyond the agreed scope without your written approval.
