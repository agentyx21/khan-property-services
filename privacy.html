Privacy

Privacy notice

A plain-language explanation of what information this website collects, why, and what we do with it.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry, we receive the details you choose to give us: your name, email address, your role, information about the property and your message. We do not ask for more than we need to reply.

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and images from Unsplash, which receive standard technical information (such as your IP address) when your browser requests those files.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a site visit or condition walkthrough.
  • To prepare a scope of work and estimate if you ask us to.
  • To keep a record of work we carry out for you, if you become a client.

We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to work are deleted once they are no longer needed to answer your questions. Records of work we carry out are kept for as long as they are useful to you and to the property, or as long as we are required to keep them.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing info@khanpropertyservices.com.

Questions

If you have any questions about this notice, email info@khanpropertyservices.com.
